# Elemental Wars - Boss Design Document

## Boss Overview
Four epic bosses representing different elemental themes with unique mechanics and strategies.

## Boss 1: Inferno Dragon

### Stats
- **Health**: 10,000 HP
- **Level**: 30
- **Element**: Fire
- **Difficulty**: Hard

### Appearance
- Massive red dragon with flame aura
- Wings span 20 meters
- Glowing magma veins across body
- Crown of fire on head

### Mechanics

#### Phase 1 (100%-66% Health)
- **Flame Burst**: Shoots fireball at player (500 damage)
- **Tail Sweep**: Spins tail dealing 400 damage (dodgeable)
- **Fire Breath**: Cone attack covering 180 degrees (600 damage)

#### Phase 2 (66%-33% Health)
- Adds **Meteor Storm**: Calls meteors from sky (300 damage each)
- Increases attack speed by 50%
- Fire Breath becomes wider (300 degree cone)

#### Phase 3 (33%-0% Health)
- **Inferno Form**: Becomes completely engulfed in flames
- All attacks deal 50% more damage
- Spawns Fire Elementals (mini-bosses)
- Ultimate: Massive explosion (1,500 damage, requires dodge)

### Strategy
- Use Water powers to reduce damage
- Attack during cooldown periods
- Dodge left/right for Flame Burst
- Jump over Tail Sweep
- Move away from Fire Breath cone

### Rewards
- Experience: 5,000 XP
- Gold: 2,000
- Item: Inferno Heart (Fire weapon upgrade)
- Achievement: "Dragon Slayer"

## Boss 2: Tidal Leviathan

### Stats
- **Health**: 12,000 HP
- **Level**: 32
- **Element**: Water
- **Difficulty**: Hard

### Appearance
- Colossal serpent made of water
- 30 meters long with multiple segments
- Glowing water orbs along body
- Constantly shifting and reforming

### Mechanics

#### Phase 1 (100%-66% Health)
- **Tidal Wave**: Creates wave covering entire arena (400 damage)
- **Water Tentacle**: Summons tentacles (300 damage each)
- **Drown**: Attempts to freeze player (requires button mashing to escape)

#### Phase 2 (66%-33% Health)
- Spawns Water Elementals
- Tidal Wave becomes faster
- Adds **Whirlpool**: Creates spinning vortex (pulls player in, 500 damage)

#### Phase 3 (33%-0% Health)
- **Tsunami Form**: Becomes a massive wave
- All attacks deal increased damage
- Arena becomes flooded
- Ultimate: Tidal Surge (2,000 damage, avoidable)

### Strategy
- Use Lightning powers to damage (water conducts electricity)
- Use Wind to push away attacks
- Dodge Tidal Waves by jumping
- Stay mobile to avoid tentacles
- Attack when tentacles retract

### Rewards
- Experience: 5,500 XP
- Gold: 2,500
- Item: Leviathan Scale (Defense upgrade)
- Achievement: "Ocean Guardian"

## Boss 3: Storm Titan

### Stats
- **Health**: 11,000 HP
- **Level**: 31
- **Element**: Lightning
- **Difficulty**: Very Hard

### Appearance
- Humanoid figure made of lightning
- 15 meters tall
- Storm clouds surrounding body
- Thunder constantly crackling
- Eyes glow bright blue

### Mechanics

#### Phase 1 (100%-70% Health)
- **Lightning Bolt**: Fires bolt at player (450 damage)
- **Thunder Clap**: Stuns nearby enemies (300 damage + stun)
- **Electrify**: Charges ground (350 damage)

#### Phase 2 (70%-40% Health)
- Adds **Chain Lightning**: Jumps between targets (250 damage each)
- Increases movement speed
- Creates Lightning Walls (blocking paths)

#### Phase 3 (40%-0% Health)
- **Overcharge Form**: Becomes supercharged
- All attacks deal 60% more damage
- Spawns Lightning Elementals
- Ultimate: Cataclysm (1,800 damage, area denial)

### Strategy
- Use Earth powers to ground electricity
- Use Water to absorb electricity
- Avoid standing still
- Dodge Lightning Bolts
- Break through Lightning Walls with strong attacks

### Rewards
- Experience: 5,000 XP
- Gold: 2,200
- Item: Storm Crown (Speed upgrade)
- Achievement: "Storm Breaker"

## Boss 4: Void Sentinel (Final Boss)

### Stats
- **Health**: 15,000 HP
- **Level**: 35
- **Element**: Void/Dark
- **Difficulty**: Legendary

### Appearance
- Humanoid shadow creature
- 12 meters tall
- Purple and black aura
- Void particles constantly emanating
- Eyes are portals to darkness

### Mechanics

#### Phase 1 (100%-75% Health)
- **Void Bolt**: Shoots dark energy (500 damage)
- **Shadow Clone**: Creates clone (mirrored attacks)
- **Darkness**: Fills arena with darkness (reduced visibility)

#### Phase 2 (75%-50% Health)
- Spawns multiple clones (up to 3)
- Adds **Void Rift**: Opens rift that damages (400 damage)
- Darkness effect stronger

#### Phase 3 (50%-25% Health)
- **Void Form**: Becomes partially intangible
- All attacks deal 70% more damage
- Multiple rifts appear
- Spawns Void Minions

#### Phase 4 (25%-0% Health)
- **Cataclysm Mode**: Ultimate attack pattern
- Combines all previous attacks
- Rapid attack sequence
- Final attack: Void Explosion (3,000 damage)

### Strategy
- Use Light/Holy powers (most effective)
- All elements work but Light is strongest
- Destroy clones quickly
- Stay near light sources
- Dodge and weave through attacks
- Team up with allies (co-op recommended)
- Use ultimate abilities strategically

### Rewards
- Experience: 10,000 XP
- Gold: 5,000
- Item: Void Essence (Legendary weapon)
- Title: "Void Slayer"
- Achievement: "Legend Achieved"
- Exclusive cosmetic skin

## Difficulty Scaling

### Easy Mode
- Boss health -30%
- Attack damage -25%
- Attack frequency -20%

### Normal Mode
- Base stats
- Recommended for level 30+

### Hard Mode
- Boss health +30%
- Attack damage +25%
- More attack combinations
- Additional minions

### Legendary Mode
- Boss health +60%
- Attack damage +50%
- Complex attack patterns
- Permanent effects
- Only for level 35+

## Boss Mechanics Guide

### Common Boss Actions
1. **Attack Phase**: Boss attacks continuously (10-15 seconds)
2. **Cooldown Phase**: Boss recovers (5-10 seconds) - optimal time to attack
3. **Special Attack Phase**: Boss uses ultimate ability
4. **Transition Phase**: Boss changes form or arena changes

### Player Mechanics
- Health system tracks damage
- Healing items restore 500 HP
- Dodge roll grants temporary invincibility
- Ultimate abilities charge during combat
- Elemental advantages provide 20% damage boost
